# ToyotaDrive Dashboard

## Purpose

The ToyotaDrive dashboard provides management with information about vehicle sales and repair operations.

## Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard tracks:

- Total vehicles sold
- Total sales revenue
- Average vehicle selling price
- Total repairs
- Total repair revenue
- Average repair cost

## Sales Analysis

The dashboard provides:

- Monthly sales revenue
- Vehicles sold over time
- Top vehicle models by revenue

## Repair Analysis

The dashboard provides:

- Monthly repair volume
- Monthly repair revenue
- Average repair cost

## Business Decisions

Management can use the dashboard to:

1. Identify sales trends.
2. Identify high-performing vehicle models.
3. Monitor repair revenue.
4. Monitor average vehicle prices.
5. Compare sales and repair performance.
6. Support inventory and service planning.
